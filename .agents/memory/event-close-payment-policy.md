---
name: Event Close payment policy
description: User-approved reimbursement, cash, late-player and rollout rules for LUDI.
---

Newly created paid events use deliberate organiser-controlled Close. Existing events retain their original payment routing and terms.

The user defined “organiser’s fee” as their own venue share if they played, not an additional commission. Cash is subtracted only from the total expected venue reimbursement, not again from online money collected. If a participant paid online and then pays cash, refund their venue contribution entirely, but not their original fees.

The maximum online reimbursement is venue cost minus the organiser’s own playing share minus cash already received. A smaller collected amount may be deliberately accepted with acknowledgement and a visible shortfall; payout is not gated by an arbitrary final paid-player count. Booking minimum-participant guarantees are a separate rule.

Late final players may have neither voted nor paid originally. Their new one-time payment is the final venue share plus fees under the event’s frozen fee terms. Preserve original voting history rather than fabricating a Yes vote from the final played roster.

**Why:** These are the user’s explicit financial choices. Existing participants must not be silently moved to a different collection or reimbursement model.

**How to apply:** Preserve these definitions when changing organiser settlement, cash controls, fee calculation or late-player payments. Keep venue-only refund/reconciliation distinct from the bank-release decision. Confirmation must disclose the actual payout, compare it with expected reimbursement, and require acknowledgement that all payments the organiser expects have arrived.

Final cost and roster are confirmed before venue refunds and final-price links. Cash can still be recorded afterward; reversing cash back into an online payer cannot undo an issued refund.

**Why:** A changed denominator or a still-payable original checkout can invalidate final-price links and previous refunds.

**How to apply:** Any future request to reopen a final roster needs an explicit financial replan, expiry of old links, and reconciliation of previous refunds—not simply an editable list.

A final venue bill may exceed the original agreed maximum contributions. Preserve those agreed caps and permit an acknowledged short payout; do not block reconciliation or charge the difference automatically.

**Why:** The user requires a monetary, flexible payout threshold with explicit disclosure of any venue shortfall.

**How to apply:** Distinguish the venue reimbursement target from each participant's payment contract. An original payer who paid their maximum remains paid even when the venue target is higher. Preserve the original booking-minimum refund guarantee separately from final payout eligibility.
