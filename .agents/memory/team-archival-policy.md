---
name: Team archival policy
description: Non-destructive archival, retained event access and recurring-series behavior.
---

Only the team owner can archive or restore. Archival freezes team and membership changes but preserves pending invitations and every historical record. Existing events, including upcoming paid events, remain usable under their existing permissions; archiving does not cancel or refund them.

**Why:** Archival is the reversible alternative to destructive deletion, not an event-cancellation or financial action.

**How to apply:** Keep event and payment permissions independent of active-team discovery. History must include primary, secondary and junction-linked events. Do not use active-only team lists to authorize access to existing events or financial history.

Pause generation of new recurring occurrences whenever a team linked to the series template is archived. Restoration allows normal expiry-based generation again, but never clears a separately suspended series.

**Why:** Old teams should stop producing new commitments without changing existing occurrences or overriding an organiser's explicit suspension.

**How to apply:** Check archival during both scheduled and manual maintenance. Keep this pause separate from the persisted suspension setting and preserve the five-occurrence, expiry-only replacement policy.
