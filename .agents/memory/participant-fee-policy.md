description: User-selected upfront payment, maximum-based fixed fees, and preservation of existing payment terms.
---

The user said: “The cost per player must be the defined cost plus the platform charge, and then plus the stripe charge.” Their example is £10 + 5% = £10.50, then 2.99% processing rounded upward = £10.82.

For flexible events the user explicitly chose “Take payment upfront, then refund the residual.” Both fee amounts must be calculated from the maximum venue share, not the final figure, and remain unchanged after a residual refund. At the example rates with no fixed processing charge, a £10 maximum and £8 final venue share means £2 refunded and £8.82 retained.

**Why:** The user selected the original fee amount and explicitly said “the platform and stripe fees should be calculated by the max and not the final figure.”

**How to apply:** Refund unused venue cost, not a proportional part of either fee. Do not apply these new terms retroactively to existing authorisations or payments. Preserve full-registration refund terms separately from residual refunds.

Freeze rates per newly created event and retain recurring-series terms rather than looking up current platform rates during settlement.

**Why:** Participants must keep the terms they agreed to even when SuperAdmin later changes the fees.

**How to apply:** Treat platform configuration as defaults for new events, not permission to reprice an existing event.
